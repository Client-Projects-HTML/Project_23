Integrating Smart Appliances With Your Ecosystem

Jane Doe

Senior Appliance Technician • Oct 12, 2023

Keeping your refrigerator running efficiently requires adjusting moisture, temperature, and keeping the internal components clean. Here is how you can ensure your fridge lasts longer.

"A well-maintained refrigerator can last up to 15 years, saving you thousands of dollars in replacement and energy costs over its lifetime."

1 Connect to Wi-Fi

Treat the coils behind or beneath your fridge with care. Vacuum them every six months to remove dust and pet hair, which allows the compressor to run less frequently.

2 Create Custom Routines

Link your smart coffee maker and lights to your morning alarm. With custom routines, your kitchen can start preparing for your day before you even get out of bed.

3 Monitor Energy Usage

Many smart appliances provide real-time energy consumption data. Use these insights through their companion apps to identify peak usage times and adjust habits accordingly.

4 Enable Remote Diagnostics

If a smart appliance breaks down, manufacturers can often run remote diagnostics via Wi-Fi. This helps technicians arrive with the correct parts, speeding up repairs.
