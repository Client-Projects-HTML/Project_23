Weekly Laundry Routine Guide For Busy Families On A Budget

Save up to $150 a year by optimizing your washer settings and using cold water cycles effectively. A solid routine not only saves money but significantly reduces the time you spend on chores.

1. Sort Smarter, Not Harder

Instead of multiple loads throughout the week, have distinct hampers for whites, darks, and heavily soiled items. This prevents color bleeding and allows you to run full, efficient loads.

2. Embrace Cold Water Cycles

Heating water accounts for about 90% of the energy your washing machine uses. Switching to cold water for everyday loads can noticeably lower your utility bills without sacrificing cleanliness.

3. Clean Your Washer

A dirty washer is an inefficient one. Run a cleaning cycle with white vinegar or a specialized cleaner once a month to remove buildup and prevent musty odors in your clothes.

4. Dry Efficiently

Clean the lint filter before every load and ensure your dryer vent is clear. Adding a dry towel to a wet load can also help reduce drying time significantly.
